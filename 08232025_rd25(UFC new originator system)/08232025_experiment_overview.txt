-----Intro:
In my last test, I compared the 3 UFC prompts that I have had in betting the same event. Mostly to see how the old scripts were
comparing to my new script. The new script was simply unable to following instructions. After some analysis I decided the amount of
specific tasks I needed Gemini to perform was simply too much for its capabilities which was leading to a loss in performance. To
solve this I decided the best way to move forward was to try to split the tasks up into smaller chunks and run them consecutively. I
created a sports betting agent prompt and through my discussiong with this prompt created a slightly different system that is going
to research the markets (agnostic of lines), originate its own lines, and then based on its own lines and the lines of a sports book
it chooses bets to make based on a percieved mispricing/edge.
