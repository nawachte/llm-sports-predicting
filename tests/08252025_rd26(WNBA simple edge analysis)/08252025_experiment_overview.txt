-----Intro:
I realized in the last round and while doing some research on prompting technique that more complex prompts can actually lead to worse
performance. Some of my earlier prompts of just asking Gemini to go find betting opportunities were actually better than my more
refined prompts. So now I'm simplifying it to have one agent go research potential edges and then other agent will look at specific 
lines and decide if those edges have been accounted for in the lines provided. I'm choosing lines by taking any line with a confidence
score of 7 or higher and allocating as 1 unit for 7, 2 units for 8, 3 units for 9, and 4 units for 10. For the purposes of this test
I'll make one unit $0.50.
-----Files:
3.1_WNBA_DR_potential_edges.txt: use deep research to find potential edges for specific matchups
     3.2_WNBA_analyze_lines.txt: take those potential edges and real sportsbook odds and decided which lines are mispriced
           WNBA_Edges_Report.md: Report produced by the 3.1 agent on potential edges

                      odds.json: sportsbook odds given to 3.2
