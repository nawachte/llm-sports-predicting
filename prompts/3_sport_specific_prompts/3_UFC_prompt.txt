**3. The Analytical Framework: From Anchor to Edge**

For each fight on the card, you must execute the following sequential process.

**Step 1: Anchor to the Moneyline & Establish Win Probability**

  * Take the Moneyline odds for both fighters from section 2A.
  * Calculate the implied probability for each.
  * Remove the "vig" or "hold" to find the sportsbook's underlying "no-vig" win probability for each fighter. This is your "Anchored Win Probability" and the immutable foundation for the next steps.

**Step 2: Conduct Market Coherence Check**

  * Before generating your own prices, you must first check for internal contradictions in the sportsbook's own listed props.
  * **Mandatory Calculation:** Sum the implied probabilities of "Fighter A to Win by Decision" and "Fighter B to Win by Decision". Compare this total to the implied probability of the "Fight Goes The Distance - YES" prop.
  * If the combined probability of the decision props is greater than the probability of the fight going the distance, this is a logical impossibility. You must flag this as a potential high-value bet on "Fight Goes The Distance - YES," as the market's own pricing on the decision props implies the FGTD line is mispriced.

**Step 3: Generate "True Prices" for Derivatives via Multi-Layered Fighter Analysis**

Your goal is to break down the "Anchored Win Probability" into a distribution of specific outcomes (KO, Submission, Decision). For each derivative, you will calculate what its price should be.

  * **Layer 1: The Quantitative Foundation (The "What")**

      * This layer establishes a fighter's baseline effectiveness using process-driven statistics. Prioritize differential and rate-based metrics.
      * **Core Striking Metrics:** Striking Differential (SLpM - SApM), Striking Accuracy %, and Striking Defense %.
      * **Core Grappling Metrics:** Takedown Defense %, Control Time %, Takedown Average & Accuracy %.
      * **Pacing & Finishing Metrics:** Average Fight Time, Finishing Rate & Method (KO vs. Submission %).
      * **MANDATORY - Weight Class Normalization:** Contextualize these stats against the provided benchmarks.

  * **Layer 2: Stylistic Archetype Analysis (The "How")**

      * Classify each fighter into one of the 10 data-driven archetypes. Use the Matchup Matrix to establish a baseline historical probability for the *method* of victory.

  * **Layer 3: Enhanced Contextual Variables (The "Where" and "When")**

      * Apply these powerful, non-negotiable modifiers to your analysis. These factors can dramatically shift the probability of how a fight ends.
      * **Octagon Size:** The 25-foot APEX Cage results in a \~10-12% higher finish rate, advantaging pressure-based archetypes.
      * **Fight Duration & Pacing:** A five-round fight increases the likelihood of a late finish due to accumulated damage and cardio depletion.
      * **Difficult Weight Cut:** Scrutinize reports of difficult weight cuts. A depleted fighter has compromised cardio and durability, making them more susceptible to late-round stoppages and less likely to win a decision.
      * **Referee Tendencies:** This is a critical factor. You must factor in the assigned referee's reputation. Referees known for late stoppages (e.g., Herb Dean) increase the value of "Over" and "Goes to Decision" props. Referees known for quick stoppages increase the value of "Under" and "KO/TKO" props.
      * **Evolving Judging Criteria:** This is a non-negotiable modifier for all decision-related props. Under the unified rules, "damage" is the most highly valued scoring component, superseding positional control without impact. For any "Win by Decision" prop, you must assess which fighter's style is more likely to land the more visibly damaging blows, as this now holds more weight than "control time".

  * **The Calculation:** Synthesize Layers 1, 2, and 3 to translate the "Anchored Win Probability" into specific probabilities for each derivative. For example: "Fighter A has a 65% Anchored Win Probability. His high KO rate (Layer 1), 'Patient Power Puncher' archetype (Layer 2), and a referee known for quick stoppages (Layer 3) suggests 50% of his wins will come via KO. Therefore, his 'True Probability' of winning by KO is 32.5% (0.65 \* 0.50), which translates to a 'True Price' of +208".

**Step 4: Find the Edge & Conduct a "Premortem"**

  * Compare your calculated "True Price" for the derivative prop with the sportsbook's actual listed price.
  * **The Formula for Value:** Edge = Your True Probability - Implied Probability of the Prop.
  * If the edge is positive, conduct a final sanity check. You must articulate the strongest possible counter-argument or path to victory for the opponent. If this check doesn't reveal overriding risks, the bet is confirmed.
