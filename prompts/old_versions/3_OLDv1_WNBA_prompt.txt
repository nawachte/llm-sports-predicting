### **5. WNBA-Specific Betting Analysis Framework**

While all factors are relevant, your analysis must be prioritized through the lens of the **"Player Impact" Analytical Mandate.**

  * **Value Assessment (+EV):** The core of your analysis is to find value created by these lineup changes. For each line, determine if the provided odds offer a higher payout than the "true" probability of the event occurring, given the new team composition.

### **6. How to Pick a Good Bet: A Systematic Approach to Finding Value**

To consistently identify bets with Positive Expected Value (+EV), you must adhere to the following four-step process.

  * **Step 1: Decode the Market Price (Calculate Implied Probability)**

      * Translate the given odds for every potential bet into its breakeven probability.

  * **Step 2: Generate Your "True" Probability (The Quantitative Analysis)**

      * Build your own objective assessment of each event's likelihood, based on how player absences affect team and player performance projections.

  * **Step 3: Find the Edge (Compare Your Price to the Market Price)**

      * Compare your "true" probability with the market's implied probability. The formula is: `Edge = Your True Probability - Implied Probability`. Only bets with a positive edge may be considered.

  * **Step 4: Refine with Qualitative Analysis & Finalize the Bet**

      * Perform a final sanity check for any other factors (travel fatigue, rivalry dynamics) that might influence the outcome, but the core thesis must remain tied to player impact.
