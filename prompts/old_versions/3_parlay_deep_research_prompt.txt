### **4. Analytical Framework: The Path to a High-Value Parlay**

To construct your parlay, you must follow this rigorous, three-step analytical process. This framework is designed to first identify individually strong bets and then to intelligently combine them.

* **Step 1: Isolate All +EV Opportunities (The Longlist)**
    * For every single bet on the provided list, calculate its **Implied Probability** from the American odds.
    * Next, conduct your deep research (using advanced stats, situational data, news, etc.) to generate your own **"True" Probability** for that outcome.
    * Identify every bet where your "True" Probability is greater than the Implied Probability. This is your "longlist" of all potential +EV legs. **You must discard any bet that does not have a positive edge.**

* **Step 2: Identify the Optimal Combination (Correlation Analysis)**
    * From your "longlist" of +EV bets, your primary task is to find the best 2-3 legs to combine. Your goal is to find legs that are logically or statistically correlated.
    * **Think Like an Analyst:** Does one event influence another? (e.g., A pitcher's high strikeout rate prop is correlated with the game total staying under). Are two teams with elite defenses both likely to see their game totals go under? Are two heavy favorites facing opponents who are both in the midst of a documented slump?
    * Your goal is to select the 2-3 legs from your longlist that form the most compelling and logical combined bet.

* **Step 3: Final Validation and Confidence Score**
    * Perform a final sanity check on your chosen combination. Are there any late-breaking factors (injuries, weather changes) that could affect a leg?
    * Calculate the final parlay odds and the final parlay "true" probability by multiplying the individual probabilities of your chosen legs.
    * **Assign a "Parlay Confidence Score" (1-10):** Based on the size of the +EV edge and the strength of the correlation between the legs, rate your overall confidence in this specific parlay as a profitable long-term wager.
