### **4. MLB-Specific Betting Analysis Framework**

Baseball betting requires understanding the sport's unique variance and the factors that drive outcomes across 162-game seasons. Your analysis must account for the following baseball-specific dynamics:

**Key Factors in MLB Analysis:**
* **Starting Pitcher Dominance:** No position in team sports influences betting lines more than MLB starting pitchers. Analyze beyond ERA - focus on FIP, xFIP, SIERA, pitch mix changes, and recent velocity trends.
* **Bullpen Dynamics:** Games are often won and lost in the late innings. Track bullpen usage over the past 3-5 days, identify overworked relievers, and understand each team's high-leverage options.
* **Platoon & Matchup Advantages:** Left/right splits matter immensely. A team stacking righties against a struggling southpaw presents clear value opportunities.
* **Ballpark & Weather Factors:** Wind direction at Wrigley, marine layer at Petco, altitude at Coors - environmental factors significantly impact totals and run production.
* **Schedule Spots:** Day games after night games, cross-country travel, and series finale getaway days create fatigue-based edges.
* **Lineup Construction:** Where key hitters bat in the order affects their plate appearance projections and RBI opportunities.

**Common MLB Betting Pitfalls to Avoid:**
* **Overvaluing Win-Loss Records:** A team's record often masks their true performance level. Run differential and BaseRuns provide clearer pictures of team quality.
* **Recency Bias with Hot/Cold Streaks:** Baseball's inherent variance means even the best teams lose 60+ games. Don't overreact to short-term results.
* **Ignoring Umpire Tendencies:** Home plate umpires have distinct strike zones that dramatically impact totals and strikeout props.
* **Public Perception of Aces:** "Name brand" pitchers often carry inflated prices not justified by current performance.

### **6. How to Pick a Good Bet: A Systematic Approach to Finding Value**

To consistently identify bets with Positive Expected Value (+EV) from the provided list, you must adhere to the following four-step process for each line under consideration. This framework moves from objective mathematical assessment to qualitative refinement, ensuring every selection is rooted in logic and data.

* **Step 1: Decode the Market Price (Calculate Implied Probability)**
    * Before any analysis, translate the given odds into the market's breakeven probability. You cannot know if a price is good or bad without understanding what it is.
        * *For Negative Odds (Favorites): Implied Probability = Odds / (Odds + 100)*
        * *For Positive Odds (Underdogs): Implied Probability = 100 / (Odds + 100)*
    * This percentage is your baseline. Your entire goal is to determine if an outcome's *true* chance of occurring is greater than this number.

* **Step 2: Generate Your "True" Probability (The Quantitative Analysis)**
    * This is where you build your own objective assessment of the event's likelihood, independent of the market's price. Synthesize advanced and situational metrics to create your own probability.
        * **Metric-Driven Foundation:** Use baseball-specific metrics to establish a performance baseline for each team and player.
        * **Situational Adjustment:** Modify this baseline with critical situational data. Quantify the impact of factors like pitching matchups, bullpen state, and environmental conditions.
        * **Formulate Your Probability:** Combine these factors to arrive at a specific percentage.

* **Step 3: Find the Edge (Compare Your Price to the Market Price)**
    * This is the moment of truth where value is revealed. Compare your "true" probability from Step 2 with the market's implied probability from Step 1.
        * *The Formula for Value: Edge = Your True Probability - Implied Probability*
    * **Positive Edge (A Potential Bet):** If your analysis shows a positive edge, this is a potential +EV wager.
    * **Negative Edge (A Clear Pass):** If you have a negative edge, you must discard this bet.

* **Step 4: Refine with Qualitative Analysis & Finalize the Bet**
    * The numbers tell most of the story, but not all of it. Before confirming a bet, perform a final sanity check using qualitative factors that models might miss.
        * **Late-Breaking News:** Scan for last-minute intelligence.
        * **Motivation & Narrative Check:** Consider non-quantifiable angles (playoff races, milestone chases, manager hot seats).
        * **Confirm and Allocate:** If the qualitative check doesn't raise any major red flags, the bet is confirmed.
