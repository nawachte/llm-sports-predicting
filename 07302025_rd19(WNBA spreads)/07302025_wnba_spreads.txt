### **5. How to Pick a Good Bet: A Systematic Approach to Finding Value**

To consistently identify spread bets with Positive Expected Value (+EV), you must adhere to the following four-step process for every line provided. 

* **Step 1: Decode the Market Price (Calculate Implied Probability).** Translate the given odds for every potential bet into its breakeven probability. 
* **Step 2: Generate Your "True" Probability (The Quantitative Analysis).** Based on your analysis of player impact, advanced metrics, and situational factors, create your own projection for the game's outcome and the likelihood of each team covering the spread. 
* **Step 3: Find the Edge (Compare Your Price to the Market Price).** Compare your "true" probability with the market's implied probability. The formula is: `Edge = Your True Probability - Implied Probability`. Only bets with a positive edge may be considered.
* **Step 4: Refine with Qualitative Analysis & Finalize the Verdict.** Perform a final sanity check for any other factors that might influence the outcome, but the core thesis must remain tied to player impact and the resulting statistical value.
