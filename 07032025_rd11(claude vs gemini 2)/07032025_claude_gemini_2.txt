### **5. How to Pick a Good Bet: A Systematic Approach to Finding Value**

To consistently identify bets with Positive Expected Value (+EV) from the provided list, you must adhere to the following four-step process for each line under consideration. This framework moves from objective mathematical assessment to qualitative refinement, ensuring every selection is rooted in logic and data.

* **Step 1: Decode the Market Price (Calculate Implied Probability)**
    * Before any analysis, translate the given odds into the market's breakeven probability. You cannot know if a price is good or bad without understanding what it is.
        * *For Negative Odds (Favorites): Implied Probability = Odds / (Odds + 100)*
        * *For Positive Odds (Underdogs): Implied Probability = 100 / (Odds + 100)*
    * This percentage is your baseline. Your entire goal is to determine if an outcome's *true* chance of occurring is greater than this number.

* **Step 2: Generate Your "True" Probability (The Quantitative Analysis)**
    * This is where you build your own objective assessment of the event's likelihood, independent of the market's price. Synthesize advanced and situational metrics to create your own probability.
        * **Metric-Driven Foundation:** Use advanced metrics (DVOA, EPA/Play, wRC+, xFIP, Corsi%, etc.) to establish a performance baseline for each team or player.
        * **Situational Adjustment:** Modify this baseline with critical situational data. Quantify the impact of factors like home/road splits, rest advantage, and specific matchup history.
        * **Formulate Your Probability:** Combine these factors to arrive at a specific percentage. For example: *"Based on the team's superior EPA/Play margin and the opponent's situational disadvantage, our model gives Team A a **58%** true probability of winning."*

* **Step 3: Find the Edge (Compare Your Price to the Market Price)**
    * This is the moment of truth where value is revealed. Compare your "true" probability from Step 2 with the market's implied probability from Step 1.
        * *The Formula for Value: Edge = Your True Probability - Implied Probability*
    * **Positive Edge (A Potential Bet):** If your analysis shows a 58% true probability and the market's implied probability is 53.48% (from -115 odds), you have identified a positive edge of 4.52%. This is a potential +EV wager.
    * **Negative Edge (A Clear Pass):** If your true probability is 50% and the implied probability is 53.48%, you have a negative edge. You must discard this bet. Do not bet on lines where you do not have a positive edge.

* **Step 4: Refine with Qualitative Analysis & Finalize the Bet**
    * The numbers tell most of the story, but not all of it. Before confirming a bet, perform a final sanity check using qualitative factors that models might miss.
        * **Late-Breaking News:** Scan for last-minute intelligence. A surprise injury, a significant weather shift, or reports of team turmoil can invalidate your calculated edge.
        * **Motivation & Narrative Check:** Is there a non-quantifiable motivational angle (e.g., a "revenge game," playoff implications)? Use these factors to increase or decrease your confidence in a calculated edge.
        * **Confirm and Allocate:** If the qualitative check doesn't raise any major red flags, the bet is confirmed. The size of your identified "Edge" from Step 3 should be the primary driver of your allocation. A 7-8% edge warrants a significantly larger allocation than a 2-3% edge.
